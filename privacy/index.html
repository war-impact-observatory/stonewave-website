We rely on your consent (when you contact us), steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, the performance of a contract, compliance with legal obligations, and our legitimate business interests.

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with:

We operate in the UAE, India and other markets, and our service providers may process data in other countries. Where information is transferred across borders, we take reasonable steps to ensure it is protected in line with applicable law.

6. How long we keep it
